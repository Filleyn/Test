# TrailGuide GPS privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

TrailGuide GPS is an offline hiking map. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and its developer runs no server that receives your data. What you make in the app stays on your phone unless you choose to send it somewhere.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your location.** The app uses GPS to show where you are, record tracks and follow routes. Location is used on the phone only. With your permission it keeps working in the background while you are recording a track or following a route, and a notification shows while it does. It stops when you stop recording or following.
- **What you save.** Routes, tracks, waypoints, notes, photos you attach, trip dates, checklists and settings are kept in the app's private storage on the phone.
- **Crash reports.** If the app closes unexpectedly, a short report is kept on the phone. It is sent to no one unless you tap Share.

## What leaves your phone, and when

The app contacts other services only for the things below. Each of these services sees your phone's internet address, as any website does, and applies its own privacy policy.

| When | What is sent | To |
|---|---|---|
| Downloading or updating maps, and checking for map updates | Which map files are requested | GitHub (github.com, api.github.com) |
| Getting a weather forecast | The coordinates of the place or route points you asked about, rounded to about 35 ft | U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) |
| Turning on the wildfire layer | A request for current fire perimeters in the states you have downloaded | National Interagency Fire Center data on ArcGIS (services3.arcgis.com) |
| Viewing or saving satellite imagery | Which map tiles are requested, which shows the area you are looking at | USGS (basemap.nationalmap.gov) and, in Oregon, Oregon Explorer (imagery.oregonexplorer.info) |
| Tapping Drive, a web link or an agency page | The place or page you tapped opens in your browser or maps app | Google Maps or the linked website |

Nothing in this table happens without a connection, and the map, routes, recording and following work without one.

## What you choose to share

Trip plans, GPX files, shared routes and backups leave the phone only when you send them, through Android's share sheet or file picker, to the app or person you pick. They can contain your routes, tracks, waypoints, notes, photos, phone number and vehicle details if you entered them.

## Android backup

If Android's backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may back up the app's routes, tracks, waypoints, notes and settings to that account so they return on a new phone. Map files, satellite imagery and photos are not included. This backup is run by Google under your Google account settings; you can turn it off in Android's settings.

## Permissions

- **Location (precise, including in the background while recording or following):** your position, track recording and off-route alerts.
- **Notifications:** the recording and download notifications and off-route alerts.
- **Camera and photos:** only when you add a photo to a waypoint or note, through Android's own camera or photo picker.
- **Internet:** the downloads and lookups listed above.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Deleting an item in the app removes it from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Copies you sent to other people or apps, and any Android backup in your Google account, are controlled there.

## Not a safety device

TrailGuide GPS is a planning and navigation aid. Maps, trails, water sources, time and water estimates and "ways out" come from public map data and can be wrong or out of date. Carry a paper map and compass, tell someone your plan, and do not rely on the app, or on a phone, for your safety.
